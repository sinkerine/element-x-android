Your messages in a thread

Design showcase for docs/features/thread-my-messages-plan.md. Mockups approximate Compound tokens and are not pixel-exact. All names and messages are synthetic. Dashed orange marks the new UI.

1 · Entry point in the existing thread view

The thread view today has a back button, a room avatar, the title “Thread” with the room name, and the timeline and composer. The new entry point only adds to the top bar.

2 · The “Your messages” sheet

A Compound ModalBottomSheet over the thread, like the read-receipt and reaction sheets. It lists your messages in this thread, newest first. Each row shows a two-line summary from the existing MessageSummaryFormatter and the date and time it was sent.

3 · After tapping a message

The sheet closes and the thread timeline scrolls to the chosen message. It centers the message and highlights it with the focus style permalink and reply jumps already use: an accent top line and a soft accent wash. As with permalink jumps today, the highlight stays until you jump to another message or leave the thread. Back closes the thread, as it does today. It does not reopen the sheet.

4 · Jumping to a message older than the loaded window

The thread opens with only the latest ~50 events loaded. Example: a thread with 140 replies, where your proposal is reply #12. The sheet loads older history into the same thread timeline. The rows it reveals are then already in the timeline, so a tap jumps directly.

5 · Android-native options (Material 3)

The same feature in the patterns Android users know from WhatsApp, Telegram and Google Messages: a contextual top app bar, a full-screen list with Material list items, and a Material 3 modal sheet. All three reuse the same data and jump logic as sections 2–4. They differ only in presentation.

5A · Step through your messages in a contextual top bar

Like “search in chat” on Android. The action switches the top bar into a contextual mode. ▲ jumps to your previous (older) message and ▼ to the next (newer) one, each centered and highlighted in place. The counter shows a + while older history remains. The list icon opens the full list (5B or 5C). The ✕ button or system Back leaves the mode and keeps the scroll position.

5B · Full-screen list (Material list items)

Like “Starred messages” in WhatsApp or Google Messages. A full-screen surface with a top app bar and back arrow, two-line list items with a leading type icon, and day section headers. Older history loads automatically as you scroll (infinite scroll), with a progress row at the end. Errors use a snackbar with Retry. Tapping a message closes the screen and jumps. System Back (with predictive back) returns to the thread unchanged.

5C · Material 3 modal bottom sheet

Section 2's sheet restyled to Material 3: 28dp top corners, a drag handle, a left-aligned title and the same list items as 5B. It opens half-expanded and drags to full height. “Load older messages” is a tonal button and loading uses a circular indicator.

What counts as “my messages”

  • Included: every message event you sent in this thread: text, notices, emotes, media, files, voice, polls, location and stickers. Includes the thread root if you started the thread.
  • Excluded: redacted messages, state or membership events, and local echoes that are still sending or failed (they have no event ID yet).
  • “You” means the current session’s user ID (TimelineItem.Event.isMine), so it includes messages sent from your other devices.

Ordering

  • Newest first (recommended): matches “jump back to something I said” and keeps the most likely target at the top. Older rows are added at the bottom as history loads.
  • Chronological is the alternative. It mirrors the timeline but puts the most recent message at the end of a growing list.

Navigation

  • Tap: the sheet closes, then the existing FocusOnEvent runs and the message is centered and highlighted.
  • Swipe down, tap the scrim or press Back: the sheet closes and the timeline doesn't move.
  • Reopening the sheet keeps the loaded history. Nothing is cached after you leave the thread.
