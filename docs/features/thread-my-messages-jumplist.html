Try: scroll the thread up a little, then tap the avatar + list button. The list opens on your previous message relative to what you're reading. Tap a row to jump: the list stays open and the timeline above shows the highlighted message. Press and hold a row to preview it. Use ↑ in the sheet header to step to older messages.
  • Entry: a mini FAB with your avatar and a list badge. It sits above the existing ⌄ jump-to-bottom button in the timeline's bottom-right corner, and only shows once you've posted in the thread. The switch above shows the alternative: a top-bar icon.
  • Container: a non-modal standard bottom sheet at about 46% height, with no scrim. The thread stays visible and scrollable above it. That's what lets the list stay open while you jump around, as in Web's non-modal popover.
  • Rows are newest first and show the time, a one-line snippet and a Root chip. A green dot means the message is visible in the timeline above the sheet. An accent bar marks the current jump target, and a “Previous” label marks where ↑ will go.
  • Count: “3+” while older history remains. The sheet loads progressively all the way to the start of the thread (linear progress bar), then shows the exact count. Android thread timelines always sit at the live end, so only backwards loading is needed.
  • Preview (instead of hover) is press and hold. If the message is off screen, a card appears above the sheet. It shows your avatar, “You · time”, Edited/Root badges, the full text (up to 8 lines) and the message before it for context. If the message is on screen, its tile in the timeline is outlined instead. Releasing ends the preview. TalkBack gets a “Preview” custom action.
  • Closing: ✕, dragging the sheet down, or system Back (predictive back closes the sheet before the thread). Scrolling the timeline keeps it open.

①b What “press and hold to preview” means

Web shows a preview when you hover over a row. Phones have no hover, so on Android you keep your finger on a row for about half a second. While your finger stays down, you see the whole message without the timeline moving. Lift your finger and the preview disappears; nothing else changes. A normal short tap still jumps. In the interactive mock above, hold the mouse button down on a row to try it.

  • Why have it? Snippets are one line. Holding lets you read a long message, or check you've got the right one, without moving the timeline away from what you're reading.
  • Downside: it's a hidden gesture, so many people won't discover it. Every row still works by just tapping (jump), so nothing is lost if they don't.
  • TalkBack: each row gets a “Preview” action in the actions menu, which reads the full message aloud.
  • Alternatives: drop preview entirely, since a tap already shows the message in context and the list stays open. Or add an explicit expand ▾ on each row that shows the full text inline.
